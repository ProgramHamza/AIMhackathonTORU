The metrics in one sentence each

Answers complete
Of the 15 challenge questions: the answering item has the right status, links to an expected source document, and is on the screen without clicking (summary panel or ranked list).
Needed items found
Of the checklist items a prompt needs, how many were listed (block 5).
Questions fully supported
Of the 15 questions: the remarker marked a passage from a right document with the right status (blocks 6/7).
Flag precision
Share of marked passages that come from a document the challenge lists as a source.
Verified fact
A fact from blocks 1-4 with an exact quote checked against the document offsets.
Hand-off check
A property that must hold between two blocks on every prompt, e.g. every citation the final step makes refers to a passage the remarker produced, and that passage equals the document text.
Identifiers replaced
Names, birth dates, IDs, case numbers, addresses, phone numbers, clinicians, facilities and places removed before a cloud call.
Time, prompt to full screen
Prompt analysis + marking passages + the final check and summary; live calls, no cache. Challenge limit 10 s.

What these numbers do not prove

  • The team wrote the test prompts, the expected items and the remarker checks; a clinician has not reviewed them.
  • Blind sets are small (12-15 prompts each), so one prompt moves a score by several points.
  • "Answers complete" is our proxy; the jury reads the answer text.
  • The hand-off checks run on the template path (no model); the LLM path is covered by the end-to-end run and the code checks in block 8.
  • De-identification is pseudonymisation; the legal notes list what a real deployment still needs.

What these numbers do not prove

  • The team wrote the test prompts and the expected answers, not doctors. A clinician should review them.
  • Blind sets are small (12–15 prompts), so one prompt moves a score by about 7 points.
  • The final-step check uses our own proxy (item, status, source document, visible), not the jury's reading of the answer text. The jury may judge wording and completeness differently.
  • Gemini answers vary a little between runs; latency depends on the network and the free-tier quota.
