The whole pipeline, end to end (report 3)

Clinical documentation app, blocks 5–8. All numbers come from the evaluation runs in backend/eval/results. Hover a bar to see its value.

The metrics in one sentence each

Answers complete
Of the 15 challenge questions: right status, linked to an expected source document and visible in the summary. Our stand-in for the jury's 'correct answer'.
Time, prompt to full screen
From typing the request to the finished screen: prompt analysis + marking passages + the final check and summary. The challenge limit is 10 s.
Needed items found (blind)
On prompts never used for tuning: how many of the needed checklist items made it onto the list.
Ran with the LLM
Share of requests where both LLM calls of the final step answered in time (the rest used the template fallback).
Role recognised
Did it understand who is asking (anaesthetist, GP, …)? Share of prompts with the right role.
Needed items found
Of all the checklist items a prompt needs (allergies, blood thinners, …), how many ended up on the checklist.
Key items ranked high
Of the items that matter most, how many were marked critical or high (not buried at the bottom).
Prompts fully covered
Share of prompts where nothing needed was missing. Stricter than 'needed items found'.
Context recognised
Did it catch the situation details: the procedure, body part, symptoms, contrast, …
Irrelevant items shown
How often an item we marked as clearly irrelevant was shown anyway (e.g. tumour receptors for an anaesthetist). Lower is better.

4. Errors found while running everything together, and fixed

ProblemWhat happenedFix
Unrelated conflicts in the summaryThe carotid left/right contradiction appeared in a dentist's summary.The remarker now records which checklist items each contradiction concerns; the summary shows a conflict only for those. All conflicts stay in the conflicts list.
Relevant conflict hiddenThe first relevance rule (shared passage only) hid the carotid conflict from the follow-up GP, who needs the side of the stenosis.Same fix: relevance comes from the remarker's item scores, not only shared passages.
Stale cached summariesAfter a code change, the summary cache still served the old answer.The cache key now includes a version number and the conflicts' items.
Evaluation crashed on quotaWhen the embedding quota ran out, the semantic evaluation stopped with an error.It now warns and measures without the embedding step.

What these numbers do not prove

  • The team wrote the test prompts and the expected answers, not doctors. A clinician should review them.
  • Blind sets are small (12–15 prompts), so one prompt moves a score by about 7 points.
  • The final-step check uses our own proxy (item, status, source document, visible), not the jury's reading of the answer text. The jury may judge wording and completeness differently.
  • Gemini answers vary a little between runs; latency depends on the network and the free-tier quota.
